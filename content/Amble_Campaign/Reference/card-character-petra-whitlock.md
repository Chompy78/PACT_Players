# Petra Whitlock

**Petra Whitlock is an NPC** — a non-player character. Petra Whitlock is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Petra Whitlock is a gnome. The species (or race) of Petra is gnome.

**Role:** Crown-appointed Governor of Stoneharbour, and the town's formal civic authority — separate from the Iron Coin Guild.

**Home:** The Governor's Hall, in Old Stone — Stoneharbour's oldest civic building.

Petra Whitlock is about 58 years old.

Small and upright, silver hair cropped short for practicality. Measured and exact, and faintly dry: she answers a question with the precise clause that covers it.

The town's customs dues and its petty court both run through her.
