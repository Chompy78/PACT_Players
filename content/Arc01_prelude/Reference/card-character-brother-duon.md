# Brother Duon

**Brother Duon is an NPC** — a non-player character. Brother Duon is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Brother Duon is a gnome. The species (or race) of Brother is gnome.

**Role:** Monk mentor, a wandering ascetic. He is one of the mentors of Amble.

Spare and unhurried, in plain grey robes, shaved head, calm economical movements.
