# Stoneharbour

Stoneharbour is the port town where Arc 2 is set, and where the party are sent after leaving Amble. It is a prosperous working port of three or four thousand people on a coast none of them had seen before. Amble is a village of three hundred; Stoneharbour is roughly ten times that.

Stoneharbour runs on the tide and on other people's cargo — grain, salt, wool and fish come off the boats into warehouses, and rope, barrels and sailcloth are made and sold back to the ships.

The Iron Coin Guild keeps a branch in Stoneharbour: a converted counting-house in the Guild Quarter, with the indentured workers' barracks on its Warehouse Row edge.

Stoneharbour has eight districts: Harbourfront (the working waterfront), Warehouse Row (storage and the trades that serve it), the Guild Quarter (paperwork, law, and the Guild branch), Market Steps (the daily market, in tiers up a wide stair), Low Quay (the poor end, in the flood line), the Ropewalks (where cordage is laid), Shrine Hill (above the town), and Old Stone (the oldest quarter).

The Governor, the Watch, the Harbour Concord and the shrine are all separate authorities from the Iron Coin Guild.
