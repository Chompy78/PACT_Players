# Blake

**Blake is an NPC** — a non-player character. Blake is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Blake is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** A man at the Low Quay warehouse in Stoneharbour who hands out small boxes of charms and beats Fenwick when he comes asking.

**Home:** The Low Quay warehouse, Stoneharbour.

In Chapter 10b a man calls 'Blake!' through the warehouse door, and Blake comes out and says he does not recognise Fenwick, who gives the name 'Benwick'. Blake leads him into an office, hands over a small wooden box ('It's tea, you idiot'), then shuts the door, leans against it and asks who he is and who sent him. When Fenwick will not say, Blake beats him.

When Moss comes in and hits him with a staff, Blake and Jack go for Moss together. Moss's bear bites and claws Blake and draws blood. Nobody died, and the party got out.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published Chapter 10b.*
