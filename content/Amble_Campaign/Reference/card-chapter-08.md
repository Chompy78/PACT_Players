# Chapter 8 — Unsafe Alone

Chapter 8 of the PACT campaign is titled "Unsafe Alone". It is the winter chapter. Asked what happened in Chapter 8, or for a summary of Chapter 8, this is that chapter. Chapter 8 and "Unsafe Alone" are the same chapter.

The player characters are 16 years old in Chapter 8.

What happened in Chapter 8: Two months of guild routine pass before Wren reports Shrine Hill's alarm bell ringing. The party helps drive off a wolf pack that had killed a calf, and afterwards learns of an older shrine elder, Hesk, who resents Wren for exposing him skimming the shrine's own donations. Wren later fails to return to the barracks overnight and is found beaten outside the guild. Barred from guild or Watch involvement, the party sets a decoy at her usual sketching spot after dark and drives off three hooded attackers who warn her to stay away from the shrine -- strangers, unmasked but not identified, though the party strongly suspects Hesk had a hand in it. Afterwards, the party's standing with Wren improves.

The player characters present in Chapter 8 are Caspian, Anders Pipeleaf, Fenwick Copperkettle, Moss Stormspud and Archer.

Skylar is not present in Chapter 8.
