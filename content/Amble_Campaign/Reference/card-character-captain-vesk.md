# Captain Vesk

**Captain Vesk is an NPC** — a non-player character. Captain Vesk is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Captain Vesk is a human. The species (or race) of Vesk is human.

**Role:** An adventurer the village shouts for by name, carrying a sword too big for anyone sensible to carry.

In Chapter 2 he walks up to one of the two trolls at the wood line as though annoyed at it, and nearly takes its arm off in one motion.
