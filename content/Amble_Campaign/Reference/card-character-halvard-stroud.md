# Halvard Stroud

**Halvard Stroud is an NPC** — a non-player character. Halvard Stroud is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Halvard Stroud is a half-orc. The species (or race) of Halvard is half-orc.

**Role:** Captain of the Stoneharbour Watch — the town's own guard, separate from the Guild.

**Home:** The Harbourfront watch house, by the customs jetty.

Halvard Stroud is about 41 years old.

Broad, and deliberately unhurried in how he moves. Level and patient, and picks his words like a man who has had the argument before.
