# Silas

**Silas is an NPC** — a non-player character. Silas is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Silas is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** A man in the Stoneharbour underworld who gave Anders a sliver in exchange for a false story.

**Home:** Stoneharbour.

In Chapter 10b Peter says Silas told his men where to find Anders near the Guild. Anders had sold Silas a story about prisoners, and taken a sliver from him in exchange.

In Chapter 10c Peter says Silas sold him Anders's debt, about a hundred and twenty gold, for forty gold, because Silas could not collect it.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published Chapters 6, 10b and 10c.*
