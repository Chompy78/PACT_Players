# Halla Storn

**Halla Storn is an NPC** — a non-player character. Halla Storn is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Halla Storn is a human. The species (or race) of Halla is human.

**Role:** Barbarian mentor, and a huntress. A woman of few words. She is one of the mentors of Amble.

Tall and rangy, weathered more by weather than by age, in furs and worn leathers, with an old hunting scar across one forearm.
