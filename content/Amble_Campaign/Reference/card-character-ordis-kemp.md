# Ordis Kemp

**Ordis Kemp is an NPC** — a non-player character. Ordis Kemp is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Ordis Kemp is an elf. The species (or race) of Ordis is elf.

Ordis is not a half-elf, a human or a halfling.

Ordis Kemp uses she/her pronouns.

**Role:** A fellow Grade I indentured worker sharing the party's barracks block in Stoneharbour.

**Home:** The barracks block, Guild Quarter, Stoneharbour.

Ordis Kemp is about 19 years old.

In Chapter 5 she gives her name as though handing over two facts she would rather have kept, and goes back to her kit.

She takes to Moss, and asks to come along to the shrine, being an elf a long way from anything green.

She is indentured like the others in the bunk room.

A drunk harasses her in the Low Tide tavern, which is how the fight there starts.
