# Old Piper

**Old Piper is an NPC** — a non-player character. Old Piper is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Old Piper is a dwarf. The species (or race) of Old is dwarf.

**Role:** The blind man who tells tall tales in the Amble tavern for coin, and the Bard mentor of Amble. Every one of his stories is rubbish, and everyone buys him a drink anyway.

In Chapter 4 his nonsense about Grey Fever includes one detail that turns out to matter: that a Silver Drop thrown down a well will clean the water, and the water will do the rest.

Anders remembers it in the middle of the chase, and throwing the pearl down the village well is what saves Amble.
