# Chapter 8b — Partial Success

Chapter 8b of the PACT campaign is titled "Partial Success". It is the winter chapter. Asked what happened in Chapter 8b, or for a summary of Chapter 8b, this is that chapter. Chapter 8b and "Partial Success" are the same chapter.

The player characters are 16 years old in Chapter 8b.

What happened in Chapter 8b: A few days after Chapter 8. On a day off at Dela's cookshop the party hear that Tam's contract has been bought and that Sena is still missing. Factor Slate then gives them a Guild contract for a lawful killing at a tannery in the Ropewalks: deal with the woman who runs it, and bring out any prisoners held there. At the tannery a child who had been talking to Fenwick is badly hurt when Moss sends his goat Brambleback at him, and the party fight the guards at the door; two of the guards die. Inside, the woman keeps getting back up until the party finally kill her, and Skylar cuts off her head. Skylar and Archer run for the Guild, while Caspian, Anders, Fenwick and Moss go into the cellar, find six chained prisoners, two of them branded by the Guild, and are beaten unconscious by a mob from the Ropewalks. They wake in the Guild's medical halls and are told they were carried there. Slate says the main objective was achieved and the prisoners recovered, but the situation with Odalys Praed is not good. Blindfolded, the party meet Odalys, who is angry that a child was hurt. She nods at Caspian, Skylar and Fenwick, and tells Anders, Archer and Moss they are not welcome in the Ropewalks except on Guild business.

The player characters present in Chapter 8b are Caspian, Skylar, Anders Pipeleaf, Fenwick Copperkettle, Moss Stormspud and Archer.
