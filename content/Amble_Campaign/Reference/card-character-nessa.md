# Nessa

**Nessa is an NPC** — a non-player character. Nessa is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Nessa is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** Jory's younger sister. She lives as a ward in Dela's household.

**Home:** Dela's cookshop, Market Steps.

In Chapter 7 she answers the door when the party come for her brother, is hurt when Caspian forces the door into her, and then fights him. She becomes a ward of Dela's household that afternoon.

In Chapter 8 she still crosses herself whenever she sees Caspian in the cookshop doorway.

In Chapter 10a she is seen at night at a smuggling landing on the Low Quay side of the river mouth, in armour and with the shrine keeper Ysolt. The party discuss taking her for questioning but do not decide.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published Chapters 7, 8, 9 and 10a.*
