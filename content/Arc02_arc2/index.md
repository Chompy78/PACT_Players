Amble is behind them. Six of them signed a seven-year indenture to the Iron Coin Guild and walked out under escort with the coin still burning on their shoulders; the seventh came anyway, unindentured, because there was nothing left at home to stay for.

Stoneharbour is a working port on a coast none of them has ever seen — bigger than Amble by more than any of them can picture, and entirely uninterested in who they used to be. Nobody there knows their names, or their families, or what they did to earn the brand. Whatever they are going to be now, they will have to become it in front of strangers.
