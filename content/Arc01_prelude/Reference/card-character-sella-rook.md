# Sella Rook

**Sella Rook is an NPC** — a non-player character. Sella Rook is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Sella Rook is a human. The species (or race) of Sella is human.

**Role:** Ranger mentor, and a road-warden. She is one of the mentors of Amble.

Lean and sun-browned, close-cropped hair, worn travel leathers, and a bow that has clearly seen more use than her sword.
