# Berrin Slate

**Berrin Slate is an NPC** — a non-player character. Berrin Slate is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Berrin Slate is a human. The species (or race) of Berrin is human.

**Role:** Factor of the Iron Coin Guild's Stoneharbour branch, and the party's direct handler — the person they deal with for briefings, statements and contract business.

**Home:** The counting-house, Guild Quarter.

Berrin Slate is about 44 years old.

Neat and unremarkable: Guild grey worn correctly, cuffs kept clean despite the ink, the iron coin pin exactly where regulation puts it.

Procedural, unhurried, never raised, and courteous. He greets people, learns their names and uses them, offers a chair, notices a cough and says so.
