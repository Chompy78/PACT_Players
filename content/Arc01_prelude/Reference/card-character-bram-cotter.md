# Bram Cotter

**Bram Cotter is an NPC** — a non-player character. Bram Cotter is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Bram Cotter is a human. The species (or race) of Bram is human.

Bram is not a halfling, a gnome, a goliath, an elf or an aasimar.

**Role:** The strongest child in Amble, and the party's rival growing up.

**Home:** A family farm on the east side of Amble.

His father is a drunk who mistreats him in public. Bram absorbs it and says nothing.

Bram Cotter is 10 years old in Chapter 1. Bram Cotter is 15 years old in Chapter 3. Bram Cotter is 17 years old in Chapter 4.

In Chapter 1 he starts a fight with Wren over his grandfather's stolen wooden charm, and by the end he thanks Archer and offers the group help if they ever need it.

In Chapter 3 he is scarred across the face rescuing Wren at the lime pits.

In Chapter 4 he wants to join the Iron Coin Guild, is refused, and stays behind in Amble when the party leaves for Stoneharbour.
