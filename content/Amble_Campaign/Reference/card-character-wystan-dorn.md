# Wystan Dorn

**Wystan Dorn is an NPC** — a non-player character. Wystan Dorn is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Wystan Dorn is a dragonborn. The species (or race) of Wystan is dragonborn.

**Role:** Stoneharbour's one properly trained spellcaster of local note, and keeper of the spell shop Dorn's Steady Hand.

**Home:** Dorn's Steady Hand, Market Steps, upper tier.

Wystan Dorn is about 46 years old.

Human-height, with muted earthen scales gone slightly dull with age. Measured and self-contained.
