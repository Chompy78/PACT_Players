# Chapter 2 — Summer: Market Day

Chapter 2 of the PACT campaign is titled "Summer: Market Day". It is the summer chapter. Asked what happened in Chapter 2, or for a summary of Chapter 2, this is that chapter. Chapter 2 and "Summer: Market Day" are the same chapter.

The player characters are 12 years old in Chapter 2.

What happened in Chapter 2: The party are twelve. Wren's mother Mara is buried. At the summer market they see the Iron Coin Guild recruiting, watch adventurers fight two trolls, and are caught in a stampede while chasing a ledger thief. That night they accidentally burn down the old cart shed and get away with it.

The player characters present in Chapter 2 are Caspian, Skylar, Moss Stormspud, Fenwick Copperkettle and Anders Pipeleaf.

Archer is not present in Chapter 2.
