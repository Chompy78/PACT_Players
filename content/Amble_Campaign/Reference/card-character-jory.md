# Jory

**Jory is an NPC** — a non-player character. Jory is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Jory is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** Nessa's older brother, from the Low Quay. He is indentured to the Iron Coin Guild.

**Home:** Stoneharbour.

In Chapter 7 the party come to his home, wrongly accusing him; Caspian forces the door, grapples him down and manacles him, and his sister Nessa becomes a ward of Dela's household.

In Chapter 8 he is indentured to the Guild like the rest of the party, with a bunk in a different barracks from Caspian's.

In Chapter 9 he is not on the party's side when Anders warns him about Nessa and Dela.

In Chapter 10a the ten gold that went toward Dela's debt turns out to have come from the Anchor Light shrine, which Jory raised from his old crew around the Low Quay. When the party suspect a spy inside the Guild, Factor Slate questions him and he knows nothing, though some of his old crew were at the smuggling landing.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published Chapters 7, 8, 9 and 10a.*
