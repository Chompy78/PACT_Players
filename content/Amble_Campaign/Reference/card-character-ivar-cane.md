# Ivar Cane

**Ivar Cane is an NPC** — a non-player character. Ivar Cane is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Ivar Cane is a half-orc. The species (or race) of Ivar is half-orc.

**Role:** Fighter mentor, and the village smith. He is one of the mentors of Amble.

Broad-built and forge-scarred, muted grey-green skin, small tusks, forearms corded from the anvil, a singed leather apron over plain clothes.
