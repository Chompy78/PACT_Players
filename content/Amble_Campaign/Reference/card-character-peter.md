# Peter

**Peter is an NPC** — a non-player character. Peter is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Peter is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** The leader of a group of well-armed smugglers who bring charms into Stoneharbour.

**Home:** Unknown.

In Chapter 10a his men catch Anders spying on a covered wagon. Anders is stabbed and shot, and Peter tells Fenwick and Bram that they were interrupting 'our business, with our charms'. He lets the party take their friend away and warns that they will have words later. His charms went to a tavern in the Ropewalks, and Anders says Peter was the one who shot him.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published Chapter 10a.*
