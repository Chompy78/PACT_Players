# Tobin Brant

**Tobin Brant is an NPC** — a non-player character. Tobin Brant is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Tobin Brant is a human. The species (or race) of Tobin is human.

**Role:** Dela Brant's teenage son, known as Toby — a dockside gossip-carrier who knows every stevedore's name.

**Home:** Market Steps and the docks.

Tobin Brant is about 14 years old.

All elbows and enthusiasm, and runs everywhere. Breathless, admiring, and desperate to be useful.
