# Hesper Tuck

**Hesper Tuck is an NPC** — a non-player character. Hesper Tuck is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Hesper Tuck is a halfling. The species (or race) of Hesper is halfling.

**Role:** Elected head of the Harbour Concord, the association of Stoneharbour's independent trade guilds.

**Home:** The Concord Hall, in Old Stone.

Hesper Tuck is about 49 years old.

Compact and brisk, a working tradesperson's hands under a plain Concord chain. Warm and quick, and wraps hard points in an anecdote — easy to underestimate.
