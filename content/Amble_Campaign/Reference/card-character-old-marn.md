# Old Marn

**Old Marn is an NPC** — a non-player character. Old Marn is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Old is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** A retired scout who watched over the children of Amble — kind, unhurried, and about the only adult who really listened when they talked.

In Chapter 1 he gives the party until nightfall to prove the injured fey creature is not the thing killing sheep, then heals it himself in the morning.
