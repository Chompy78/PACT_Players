# Stoneharbour Map

The town as you'd draw it for someone who had never been: the water at the top, the streets climbing away
from it, and the wall across the back with Shrine Hill standing outside on its own ground.

![[stoneharbour-town-map.png|Stoneharbour, and its districts|800]]

Low Quay is lowest and nearest the water, and floods — the river comes in at that end of the Sound and the
tide pushes the other way. Up the slope are the Ropewalks, the Market Steps and the Guild Quarter, and Old
Stone sits highest of anything inside the walls. Kingshold, the Crown's fort, and the Naval Wharf hold the
far end of the waterfront, and neither of them is anybody's business but the Crown's.

## What it looked like coming in

This is the view from the deck, the morning you arrived: the chain across the Narrows, the fort above the
wharf, the quays stacked with other people's cargo, and the whole town going up the hill behind it.
