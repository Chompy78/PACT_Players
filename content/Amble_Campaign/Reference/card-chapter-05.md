# Chapter 5 — The Rounds

Chapter 5 of the PACT campaign is titled "The Rounds". It is the late winter chapter. Asked what happened in Chapter 5, or for a summary of Chapter 5, this is that chapter. Chapter 5 and "The Rounds" are the same chapter.

The player characters are 16 years old in Chapter 5.

What happened in Chapter 5: The party leave Amble indentured to the Iron Coin Guild for seven years and 750 gold each, and travel about three weeks to Stoneharbour with Wren. They are put in the Grade I barracks and meet Corley Finn, Sena Voll, Tam Reed and Ordis Kemp. They learn guild work under Hobb Hobbern. Their first monthly statement clears nothing at all. A letter comes from Bram in Amble. A night at the Low Tide tavern ends with Moss throwing a drunk out the door. Factor Slate then sets them a courier round, and one item on the manifest is not a delivery: they repossess Old Sabine's net-mending frame for a debt. In a lane off Warehouse Row two young thugs try to take the satchel, and Moss's goat Brambleback kills one of them. The guild records it as a fatality and puts a price on it. The chapter ends at a dance.

The player characters present in Chapter 5 are Skylar, Moss Stormspud, Fenwick Copperkettle, Caspian, Anders Pipeleaf and Archer.
