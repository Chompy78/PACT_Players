# Nell

**Nell is an NPC** — a non-player character. Nell is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Nell is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** Old Corr's apprentice, and the one who would have inherited his practice.

In Chapter 4 she is out on the outlying farms when Grey Fever reaches Amble, and rides back exhausted.

She is the one who names the disease, explains that it spares children, and mentions the magical pearl called a Silver Drop.

She chooses to stay in Amble and look after Mother Yela rather than leave.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published chapters 1-4.*
