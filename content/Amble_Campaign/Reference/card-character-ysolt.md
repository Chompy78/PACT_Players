# Ysolt

**Ysolt is an NPC** — a non-player character. Ysolt is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Ysolt is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** The keeper of the Anchor Light, the sailors' shrine in the Low Quay. She can do a little healing magic.

**Home:** The Anchor Light, Low Quay.

In Chapter 10a the Anchor Light flashes its long lights to signal the smuggling boats, and Ysolt and Nessa are seen at the landing and later overseeing a delivery to a warehouse in the Low Quay. Anders recognises her as the old woman who had been questioned before.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published Chapter 10a.*
