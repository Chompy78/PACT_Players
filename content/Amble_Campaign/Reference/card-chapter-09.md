# Chapter 9 — Watch and Learn

Chapter 9 of the PACT campaign is titled "Watch and Learn". It is the winter chapter. Asked what happened in Chapter 9, or for a summary of Chapter 9, this is that chapter. Chapter 9 and "Watch and Learn" are the same chapter.

The player characters are 16 years old in Chapter 9.

What happened in Chapter 9: Factor Slate sends the party to escort Guild Agent Blane while she serves a debt-default notice, and the debtor turns out to be Dela, the cook-shop owner who has fed the party for free since they arrived in Stoneharbour. Forced to inventory her goods rather than take them, the party finds an illegal sliver at the back of her storeroom -- a bottle holding a piece of someone's life -- which turns out to be a keepsake from her own grandfather. On the walk back, Ropewalks men corner them over the signed paperwork and take their daggers rather than risk a real fight. The sliver is destroyed at Dela's own request, and the party spends the rest of the day working the town on her behalf -- a shrine donation, a round of the local shopkeepers, an anonymous fifty-six gold parcel -- until her account clears enough that she can say the number out loud without crying.

The player characters present in Chapter 9 are Caspian, Skylar, Anders Pipeleaf, Fenwick Copperkettle, Moss Stormspud and Archer.
