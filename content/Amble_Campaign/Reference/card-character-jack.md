# Jack

**Jack is an NPC** — a non-player character. Jack is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Jack is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** A man at the Low Quay warehouse in Stoneharbour who comes running when he is called, to help beat the party.

**Home:** The Low Quay warehouse, Stoneharbour.

In Chapter 10b a man yells 'Jack, get in here!', and Jack bursts into the office with a cudgel shouting 'Bloody hell, a bear!'. He and Blake go for Moss together and each land a blow as Moss carries Fenwick out. Later, one of the men tells Jack to deal with Anders. Afterwards Moss wants to go and find Jack, or Blake, 'whichever of them it had been'.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published Chapter 10b.*
