# Ilsabet Marrow

**Ilsabet Marrow is an NPC** — a non-player character. Ilsabet Marrow is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Ilsabet Marrow is a half-elf. The species (or race) of Ilsabet is half-elf.

**Role:** Records clerk at the Guild's Stoneharbour branch — the counter where information is bought.

**Home:** The Records window, counting-house ground floor.

Ilsabet Marrow is about 34 years old.

Precise and spectacled, with an immaculate ledger-hand and everything on her counter squared to the edges.

Polite and even, and quietly apologetic about the charges — and she never once waives one.
