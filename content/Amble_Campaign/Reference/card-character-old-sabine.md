# Old Sabine

**Old Sabine is an NPC** — a non-player character. Old Sabine is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Sabine is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** A net-mender in Low Quay, Stoneharbour, who had fallen behind on a guild-backed loan.

**Home:** A net-mending room on Low Quay, Stoneharbour.

In Chapter 5 the party's courier round includes a 'marked item for collection' at her address. It is not a delivery: the item is her net-mending frame, and they are there to take it for the debt.

She is old and small and smiling when she opens the door, and she shows the party her hands — knuckles going stiff after forty years of net-mending.

She does not watch them carry the crate out. As the party leave they hear her crying through the door, and a neighbour knocking and calling her name.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published Chapter 5.*
