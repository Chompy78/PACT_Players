You live in a barracks in the Guild Quarter. Most of what is interesting happens after the gate.

Read the box your character qualifies for — see [[What_Your_Character_Knows|What Your Character Knows]].

> [!info]- ⚪ COMMON — no proficiency needed
> The town does not sleep so much as change over. The Harbourfront works whatever hour the tide is, and
> the Herring Hall is busiest before dawn. Warehouse Row is dead by full dark and nobody good is in it.
>
> **Lamps are where money is.** Market Steps and the Guild Quarter are lit. Low Quay is not, and the
> Ropewalks are lit at one end only.
>
> **The Watch walks in pairs** and they walk the routes that have complaints. Stroud's people are not
> looking for you. They are looking for noise, fire and anything being carried by two people who keep
> stopping.
>
> **Warehouse Row keeps dogs.** Everybody knows this, including the people who go there anyway.
>
> Being out at night is not a crime. Being out at night with something under your coat is a conversation
> you will not enjoy.

> [!tip]- 🔵 TRAINED — proficient in **Stealth** and **Perception**
> The Watch changes over at midnight, and the hour either side is the thinnest cover in town: two shifts
> in the streets at once, and all of them awake.
>
> The quiet hour is the one before dawn. The night people have gone in and the day people have not come
> out.
>
> **Move along the water, not through the districts.** The Harbourfront is noisy, busy and full of people
> who do not care, which is better cover than an empty street where you are the only thing moving.
>
> Dogs are a bigger problem than guards and they cannot be reasoned with. The mastiffs on Warehouse Row
> are tied at four points and the gaps between are known to everybody who has ever tried.
>
> **Low Quay has no lamps and no Watch, and that is not the same as being safe.** People there notice
> strangers immediately, because everybody else is somebody they know.

> [!warning]- 🟣 RARE — **expertise in both Stealth and Perception**
> The Watch's routes are set by complaint, so they change. A district that has been quiet for a month gets
> walked once a night, and a district that had a fire gets walked four times.
>
> That means what was safe last week is not information, it is a guess. The reliable version is to know
> what has happened recently, which is what the Cistern Steps is for.
>
> The Guild's own security do not patrol. They sit, and they sit where the goods are, and they are looking
> at doors rather than streets.
>
> **The one route nobody watches** is the roofline between the Ropewalks and the back of Warehouse Row.
> There is a reason the people who use it never mention it, and you have just been told.
