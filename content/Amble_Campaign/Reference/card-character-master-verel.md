# Master Verel

**Master Verel is an NPC** — a non-player character. Master Verel is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Master Verel is a half-elf. The species (or race) of Master is half-elf.

**Role:** Wizard mentor, and a scholar. He is one of the mentors of Amble.

Ink-stained fingers, precise features, robes a size too formal for village life, faintly pointed ears the only visible sign of the elf in him.
