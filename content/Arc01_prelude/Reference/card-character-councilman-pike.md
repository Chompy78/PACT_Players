# Councilman Pike

**Councilman Pike is an NPC** — a non-player character. Councilman Pike is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Pike is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** A councilman of Amble, living in a house bigger and finer than its neighbours.

In Chapter 2 he lectures Bram about respect while saying nothing to anyone else, and Wren calls him a goody-two-shoes behind his back.

In Chapter 3 he is one of the four faces drawn in Wren's second sketchbook, and he takes Halloway's confession at the end.

In Chapter 4 he falls ill with Grey Fever.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published chapters 1-4.*
