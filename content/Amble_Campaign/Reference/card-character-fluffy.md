# Fluffy

**Fluffy is an NPC** — a non-player character. Fluffy is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Fluffy is a dog. The species (or race) of Fluffy is dog.

Fluffy is not a human, a halfling or an elf.

**Role:** A dog who hangs about the Brant cookshop on the Market Steps in Stoneharbour.

**Home:** Around the Brant cookshop, Market Steps, Stoneharbour.

In Chapter 5 he takes to Moss Stormspud immediately, on the party's first courier round.

He belongs to nobody. He is not Dela's and not the child's; he hangs about because Dela feeds him.

His name is Fluffy. The party learn it from the child of about twelve who comes to haul him off Moss by the collar.

Dela suggests the party adopt him. Skylar rules it out, on the grounds that you do not steal a child's dog.
