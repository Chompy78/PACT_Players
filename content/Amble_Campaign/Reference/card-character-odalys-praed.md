# Odalys Praed

**Odalys Praed is an NPC** — a non-player character. Odalys Praed is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Odalys is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** The person Stoneharbour's organised underworld belongs to.

**Home:** Stoneharbour.

The party first hear her name in Chapter 5, on the night of the dance, after the killing in the lane off Warehouse Row.

People say her name quietly, even in the open. The party are told the underworld belongs to her the way the Stoneharbour branch belongs to Factor Slate.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published Chapter 5.*
