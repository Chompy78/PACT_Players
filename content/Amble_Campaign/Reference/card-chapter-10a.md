# Chapter 10a — The Landing

Chapter 10a of the PACT campaign is titled "The Landing". It is the summer chapter. Asked what happened in Chapter 10a, or for a summary of Chapter 10a, this is that chapter. Chapter 10a and "The Landing" are the same chapter.

The player characters are 17 years old in Chapter 10a.

What happened in Chapter 10a: About a year and a half into the Guild indenture, the party spends a few days of downtime on their own affairs. Caspian pushes for an extra Guild job and takes ten gold off his debt, Anders takes three gold off his, and Fenwick works unpaid at the bakery and soup kitchen. A new group of indentured recruits arrives at the Guild, and Bram, from Amble, is among them, with a two-hundred-gold debt and a three-year indenture. A hanging takes place in Stoneharbour. Factor Slate then sends the party, with Bram and Wren, to watch a night-time smuggling landing at a river mouth on the Low Quay side, and to take someone of importance alive without bringing them back to the Guild. From a blind they see the boats come in, with Nessa and the Anchor Light shrine keeper Ysolt at the landing. Anders goes to look at a covered wagon, is caught, bluffs his way free, and is then stabbed and shot by men who turn out to belong to Peter's group, and is left alive. The party take no prisoners. Peter's group say they are bringing in charms; the Low Quay group only say gear. Slate questions Jory, who knows nothing. The party discuss taking Nessa but do not decide, and Moss quietly keeps a crate he took, which holds brandy and pipe tobacco.

The player characters present in Chapter 10a are Caspian, Skylar, Anders Pipeleaf, Fenwick Copperkettle and Moss Stormspud.

Archer is not present in Chapter 10a.
