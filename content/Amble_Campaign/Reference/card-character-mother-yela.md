# Mother Yela

**Mother Yela is an NPC** — a non-player character. Mother Yela is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Mother Yela is a human. The species (or race) of Mother is human.

**Role:** The woman who runs the village shrine in Amble. She is the Cleric mentor of Amble — healer and hearth-witch. She is one of the mentors of Amble.

She speaks at Mara's funeral in Chapter 2, and takes Wren in afterwards.

In Chapter 3 she sends Skylar up to Wren's bell loft, a place she has never climbed to herself.

In Chapter 4 she is one of the first to fall to Grey Fever, and she dies of it.
