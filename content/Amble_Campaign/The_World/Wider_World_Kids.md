Ask any kid in Amble what's past the crossroads and you'll get a shrug and a story, not a map. Most people who grow up here live their whole lives within a day's walk of the Council Tree — the world outside is something you hear about, not something you've seen.

## The Roads

Amble sits on a real road, not a dead end — the kind that brings peddlers, traders, and the occasional lost soul through on their way to somewhere bigger. Market days draw the most of them: cloth merchants, herb-sellers, and the odd trader with something rarer than usual in their cart. A repeat trader gets treated half like a local; a new face is watched a little closer, at least for the first visit.

## The Iron Coin Guild

Every so often, Guild agents pass through — recognisable by the small iron coin each of them wears. Sometimes they're recruiters, taking applications at a table from anyone looking for steady work and willing to sign for it. Sometimes they're something closer to heroes: skilled fighters and healers who'll deal with a problem too big for the village to handle alone, the way they did during [[The_Story_So_Far/Arc01_prelude/chapter-02-draft-Summer-Market-Day|one particularly bad Midsummer Fair]]. Amble is glad to see them and never entirely relaxed about it — everyone's heard a story about a Guild contract that cost more than it paid.

## Rumor and Change

There's a feeling, lately, that the world outside is paying Amble more attention than it used to — nothing anyone can point to exactly, just travelers who linger a little longer, questions that get asked a little more carefully. The stories the village hasn't fully understood yet are still being written.
