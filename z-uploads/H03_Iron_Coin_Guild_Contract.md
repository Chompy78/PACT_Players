# Iron Coin Guild — Standard Indenture Agreement

## Player Handout Draft

> **GM note — the member name is an example, swap it before play.** Per the party-agnostic principle
> (`D-2026-07-30-party-agnostic-design`), this project's design content doesn't name specific PCs. This
> document is the exception that proves the rule: it is a *filled-in* contract, and a blank name field would
> destroy the thing that makes it land — it has to read like a real document that a real institution has
> already processed. So it carries a specimen name, **Anders Tealeaf of Amble**, who is nobody: not a PC, not
> an NPC, and not canon. Replace it with the actual PC's name when you hand this out (three places: the
> header block, section 1, and the signature at the end), or hand it over as-is if you want the party to see
> the standard form before their own is drawn up. (Name changed from a real PC's 2026-08-03 in the Arc02
> degenericising sweep.)

# IRON COIN GUILD
## STANDARD INDENTURE AGREEMENT
### Apprentice Contract – Grade I

**Contract Number:** ICG-SH-16-4721  
**Member Classification:** Indenture Grade I  
**Member Name:** Anders Tealeaf  
**Place of Origin:** Amble, South March  
**Issuing Office:** Stoneharbour Branch  
**Contract Status:** Active  
**Date of Signing:** 14th Day of Winterfall, 1167  

---

## 1. PARTIES

This agreement is entered into between the Iron Coin Guild (“the Guild”) and:

**Anders Tealeaf of Amble**

(“the Member”)

of Amble, South March.

---

## 2. PURPOSE

The Guild agrees to provide:

- Employment placement
- Training and development
- Lodging
- Meals
- Access to approved medical services
- Opportunities for advancement

The Member agrees to provide labour, service, and reasonable obedience to lawful Guild directives.

---

## 3. TERM OF SERVICE

The Member shall serve the Guild for a period of:

**Seven (7) Years**

beginning upon arrival at their assigned Guild office.

Periods of imprisonment, desertion, unauthorised absence, or suspension may be excluded from this calculation.

---

## 4. INITIAL DEBT

The Guild records the following debt against the Member’s account:

**750 GP**

This debt reflects:

- Recruitment
- Transportation
- Placement services
- Training allocation
- Administrative onboarding

The debt shall be maintained upon the Guild Ledger.

---

## 5. COMPENSATION

The Member shall receive compensation according to:

- Assigned duties
- Risk rating
- Performance
- Guild requirements

Compensation schedules are subject to change without amendment to this agreement.

---

## 6. LODGING AND BOARD

The Guild shall provide:

- Shared lodging
- Meals
- Laundry facilities
- Access to approved medical care

Associated charges may be deducted from earnings according to current Guild schedules.

---

## 7. APPLICATION OF EARNINGS

Unless otherwise requested and approved in writing:

Any surplus earnings remaining after deductions shall automatically be applied toward the reduction of the Member’s recorded debt.

---

## 8. CONDUCT

The Member shall:

- Obey lawful Guild directives
- Protect Guild property
- Maintain required records
- Refrain from conduct harmful to Guild interests

Repeated violations may result in disciplinary action.

---

## 9. RECORDS

The Guild shall maintain an official copy of this agreement.

One certified copy shall be issued to the Member’s registered household of record.

Replacement copies may be obtained according to current Guild Records schedules and fees.

---

# PAGE 2

## 10. PLACEMENT

The Guild may assign the Member to:

- Settlements
- Branch offices
- Vessels
- Commercial ventures
- Security details
- Expeditions
- Other duties as required

Assignments may be altered without amendment to this agreement.

---

## 11. RANK

The Member enters service as:

**Indenture Grade I**

The Guild reserves the right to promote, demote, transfer, or reassign the Member according to operational requirements.

---

## 12. ADMINISTRATION ASSESSMENTS

Members may incur recurring assessments relating to:

- Record maintenance
- Assignment management
- Career administration
- Archive services
- Arbitration services
- Placement services

Current schedules are maintained by local Guild offices.

---

## 13. MEDICAL SERVICES

Emergency treatment shall not be withheld due to inability to pay.

Associated costs may be recorded against the Member’s ledger.

---

## 14. EQUIPMENT

Guild-issued equipment remains Guild property unless otherwise recorded.

Loss or damage may result in charges against the Member’s account.

---

## 15. APPEALS AND DISPUTES

Disputes regarding:

- Ledger balances
- Assessments
- Placements
- Promotions
- Contract interpretation

must be filed through the local Guild office.

Applicable fees may apply.

---

## 16. ARCHIVE SERVICES

The Guild Archive Office may provide:

- Certified copies
- Debt statements
- Ledger histories
- Contract records
- Contract review appointments

Current fees apply.

---

# PAGE 3

## 17. COMPLETION OF SERVICE

Upon completion of the Service Term, the Member shall be released from mandatory service unless otherwise contracted.

---

## 18. REPAYMENT OF DEBT

The Member may make voluntary payments against recorded debt at any time.

Debt balances may be requested from Guild Records.

Current fees may apply.

---

## 19. CLARIFICATION REGARDING DEBT

**Repayment of recorded debt does not, by itself, terminate the Service Term established under Section 3.**

---

## 20. EARLY TERMINATION

The Member may petition for early release from service.

Approval is not guaranteed.

---

## 21. EARLY RELEASE ASSESSMENT

Approved petitions may require settlement of:

- Outstanding debt
- Administrative closure costs
- Placement recovery costs
- Training recovery costs
- Other obligations arising under this agreement

Current schedules are available from Guild Records.

---

## 22. RECORDS FEES

Administrative services, including but not limited to:

- Debt statements
- Ledger histories
- Certified copies
- Archive searches
- Contract reviews
- Appeal filings

may incur fees according to current Guild schedules.

---

## 23. FINAL AUTHORITY

Interpretation of this agreement shall rest with duly authorised representatives of the Iron Coin Guild.

---

## Key Intended Contract Reveals

The first things players will notice:
- Seven-year term.
- 750 GP starting debt.
- Guild provides lodging, meals, work, and training.
- Earnings apply to debt by default.

Things they may miss:
- Debt repayment does not end the seven-year term.
- Administrative services cost money.
- Debt statements cost money.
- Contract copies cost money.
- The buyout amount is not listed.
- Early release is a petition, not a right.
