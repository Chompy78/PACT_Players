# Old Corr

**Old Corr is an NPC** — a non-player character. Old Corr is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Old is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** The village healer of Amble, who patched the party up after every scrape from the age of eight onwards.

He works with the same unhurried patience whether the injury is a scraped knee or a slashed face.

In Chapter 4 he catches Grey Fever and dies of it, the morning after Anders carries him through the snow to the shrine.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published chapters 1-4.*
