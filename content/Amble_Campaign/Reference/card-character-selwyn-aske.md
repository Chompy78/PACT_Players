# Selwyn Aske

**Selwyn Aske is an NPC** — a non-player character. Selwyn Aske is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Selwyn Aske is an elf. The species (or race) of Selwyn is elf.

**Role:** Keeper of Stoneharbour's public shrine on Shrine Hill.

**Home:** The public shrine, Shrine Hill.

Weathered and spare, in plain grey robes gone soft with washing. Plain and unhurried, and comfortable with long silences.

He looks to be in his sixties and is genuinely older, in the unhurried way of Halden elves.

In Chapter 5 he keeps the shrine out on the green fringe of Stoneharbour, where Moss spends his one day off a week.
