# The Party

There are six player characters, plus Wren, who travels with them from the end of Chapter 4.

- Caspian - player character, played by Christen. An aasimar, apprenticing as a Druid.
- Skylar - player character, played by Sam. A human, apprenticing as a Sorcerer.
- Moss Stormspud - player character, played by Josh. A goliath, apprenticing as a herbalist.
- Fenwick Copperkettle - player character, played by Rhys. A gnome, a Fighter and Rogue.
- Anders Pipeleaf - player character, played by Andrew. Half-halfling and half-gnome, a Rogue.
- Archer - player character, played by Kendall. An elf, from an undertaker family.
- Wren - an NPC, not a player character. A tiefling, and the party's closest friend.

Those six are the complete list of player characters. Everyone else in the campaign is an NPC.
