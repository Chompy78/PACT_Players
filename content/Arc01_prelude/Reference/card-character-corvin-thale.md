# Corvin Thale

**Corvin Thale is an NPC** — a non-player character. Corvin Thale is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Thale is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** A senior Iron Coin Guild official — older, better dressed, and drinking wine in a village where nobody drinks wine.

In Chapter 4 he reads out the indenture contract and brands the six player characters, and it is clear he has done this before, in other villages, to other people's children.

He is the one who says the stolen cure was meant for a town of ten thousand people.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published chapters 1-4.*
