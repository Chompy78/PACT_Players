# Yalen Fane

**Yalen Fane is an NPC** — a non-player character. Yalen Fane is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Yalen Fane is a tiefling. The species (or race) of Yalen is tiefling.

**Role:** Sorcerer mentor, young, with a knack for strange luck. He becomes Skylar's mentor at the end of Chapter 3. He is one of the mentors of Amble.

Small dark horns, a quick easy grin, prone to a faint harmless spark or crackle when startled.
