# Lovis

**Lovis is an NPC** — a non-player character. Lovis is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Lovis is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** A stores clerk at the Iron Coin Guild in Stoneharbour, who does not like Anders.

**Home:** The Guild stores, Stoneharbour.

In Chapter 8b Anders writes his name in the stores ledger and rules a line through the space where the items would go, so nobody can add to it afterwards. The clerk who reads this is Lovis, and he does not enjoy it.

Later that week the barracks hear that Lovis is not particularly good friends with Fred.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published Chapter 8b.*
