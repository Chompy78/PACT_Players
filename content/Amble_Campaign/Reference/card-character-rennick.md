# Rennick

**Rennick is an NPC** — a non-player character. Rennick is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Rennick is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** The miller of Amble, reliably rude and reliably unhelpful.

In Chapter 3 he is one of the four faces in Wren's second sketchbook, and tells the party nothing useful when they come asking about her.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published chapters 1-4.*
