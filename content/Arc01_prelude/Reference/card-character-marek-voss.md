# Marek Voss

**Marek Voss is an NPC** — a non-player character. Marek Voss is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Marek Voss is a half-elf. The species (or race) of Marek is half-elf.

**Role:** The Iron Coin Guild man who passes through Amble a few times a year, recruiting with a table and a ledger.

In Chapter 2 he takes on a village boy and brands him, and later refuses to help chase the ledger thief.

He never remembers Fenwick's name, however many times Fenwick asks.

In Chapter 3 he gives the party the one useful lead of the day, and afterwards says 'well done' — grudgingly.

In Chapter 4 he chases the party but never harms them, and afterwards gives each of them a second, unbranded iron coin to carry.
