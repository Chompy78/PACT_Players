# Chapter 7 — The Debts We Left Behind

Chapter 7 of the PACT campaign is titled "The Debts We Left Behind". It is the harvest month chapter. Asked what happened in Chapter 7, or for a summary of Chapter 7, this is that chapter. Chapter 7 and "The Debts We Left Behind" are the same chapter.

The player characters are 16 years old in Chapter 7.

What happened in Chapter 7: A storm floods Low Quay and the party are sent to help. They pull Sabine free of her flooding home, then mistake a stranger saving a soup kitchen's stock for looting before realising their mistake. They board the grounded ship the Kestrel to fight off something living in its bilge. On the way back, a fallen rafter has pinned Della's boy Toby in his own flooding kitchen, and freeing him costs Fenwick his rolling pin and Moss the last of his strength. A warehouse theft investigation turns up a net-hauler named Jory Vann, stealing to fund his sister Nessa's training and to repair the storm-damaged Anchor Light shrine, kept by Ysolt. The party turn him in; he is indentured, and Nessa becomes a ward of Della's household alongside Toby. Along the way, Anders frames an innocent guild member, Fred, out of pure frustration.

The player characters present in Chapter 7 are Moss Stormspud, Caspian, Fenwick Copperkettle and Anders Pipeleaf.

Skylar and Archer are not present in Chapter 7.
