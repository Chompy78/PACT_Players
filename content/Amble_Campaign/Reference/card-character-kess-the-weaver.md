# Kess the Weaver

**Kess the Weaver is an NPC** — a non-player character. Kess the Weaver is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Kess the Weaver is a halfling. The species (or race) of Kess is halfling.

**Role:** Rogue mentor, who deals in cloth and gossip and already knows everything. She is one of the mentors of Amble.

Small and quick-fingered, dyed threads permanently under her nails, sharp eyes that miss nothing.
