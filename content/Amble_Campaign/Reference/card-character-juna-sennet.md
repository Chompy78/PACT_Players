# Juna Sennet

**Juna Sennet is an NPC** — a non-player character. Juna Sennet is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Juna is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

Juna Sennet uses she/her pronouns.

**Role:** A sailmaker's apprentice at Canvas & Batten, the sail loft above the keel yard in Stoneharbour.

**Home:** The sail loft at Canvas & Batten, Stoneharbour.

In Chapter 5 she signs for a parcel on the party's courier round and fetches them mugs of cold water without being asked.

She is the one who notices Wren is upset and asks why, in front of everyone.

She later calls at the barracks to ask whether the party are still coming to the dance, meets them at the gate, and takes them in.

She is articled to a trade rather than indentured to the guild.
