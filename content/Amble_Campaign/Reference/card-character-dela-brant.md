# Dela Brant

**Dela Brant is an NPC** — a non-player character. Dela Brant is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Dela Brant is a human. The species (or race) of Dela is human.

**Role:** Keeper of the Brant cookshop on Market Steps, who feeds the Guild's indentured workers cheap.

**Home:** The Brant cookshop, Market Steps.

Dela Brant is about 52 years old.

Warm and tired, flour to the elbows, with a cook's forearms and a cook's memory for who likes what. Widowed, and runs the shop with her son.

Brisk kindness that never asks for thanks. She remembers what each of them orders without being told.

In Chapter 5 she feeds the party free bowls of hot food on their first courier round, over her server's objections, and offers them a standing arrangement: a meal for a bit of work, or a guild discount.
