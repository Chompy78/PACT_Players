# Chapter 10c — The Warning Light

Chapter 10c of the PACT campaign is titled "The Warning Light". It is the summer chapter. Asked what happened in Chapter 10c, or for a summary of Chapter 10c, this is that chapter. Chapter 10c and "The Warning Light" are the same chapter.

The player characters are 17 years old in Chapter 10c.

What happened in Chapter 10c: The afternoon after the night's rest, the party is back at the Iron Coin Guild with the Low Quay smugglers due the next night. Moss, who drank the brandy and smoked the cigars, cannot sleep. Factor Slate gives the party a second, shorter briefing: the Guild wants prisoners from the smugglers' landing, at least four and preferably twelve. At the Guild stores the quartermaster, Hob, tells Anders that a 'paper error' has cancelled his stores and meal entitlements, and the party works out how to take captives, drawing a cart, a mule and manacles from the stores. Peter's group tells Anders and Moss that a crate is missing and that Anders is not the one who took it; Peter gives Moss a token. Odalys Praed, in her room, talks to Moss, Anders and Caspian about debts and agrees to rescind Caspian's contract for ninety gold. The party plans the night: Moss and Caspian wait under the jetty for the boats, while Anders, Bram and Skylar, with a wagon and mule, wait near the Anchor Light shrine. When Ysolt and Nessa come out of the shrine, Anders and Bram ambush them. Bram and Anders punch Nessa, Bram knocks her down, Anders threatens to kill her unless Ysolt puts on the manacles, and Ysolt answers with spirits that burn and with commands. Bram is downed, and Ysolt stabilises him and takes Nessa back inside, leaving Bram in the street. The shrine's light flashes, no boats come, and the party gives up for the night. Nobody dies and nobody is captured. At first light Slate tells the party, drily and without raising his voice, that they failed to bring back the four prisoners he asked for, reminds them of their earlier good work, and says that some other branch will surely buy out their contracts and take them off his hands.

The player characters present in Chapter 10c are Caspian, Skylar, Anders Pipeleaf and Moss Stormspud.

Fenwick Copperkettle and Archer are not present in Chapter 10c.
