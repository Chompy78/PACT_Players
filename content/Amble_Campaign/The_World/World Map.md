# World Map

Nobody in Amble owns a map like this. What follows is roughly what a well-travelled adult would sketch for
you if you asked — close enough to argue over, and wrong in places.

![[world-map-the-continent.png|The continent|850]]

**Halden** is the big one in the middle, and it is where you have always lived. **Aeloria** lies west
beyond a long belt of old forest, **Aurelan** south-west across the marshes, and **Kaldrun** south over the
Greyback Mountains — cold, rich in ore, and not a place anyone recommends. **Albric** is the island off the
coast, close enough to trade with constantly and quarrel with occasionally.

## The Kingdom of Halden

![[world-map-kingdom-of-halden.png|The Kingdom of Halden|850]]

**Amble** is in the South March, in the hills, on the Hollin. The road south follows that river down through
**Faircross** to the coast and **Stoneharbour** — two to three weeks by wagon, as you now know better than
most. **Aldermoot**, the capital, sits north of you on the Alder; **Fordwater** is east, where the inland
road crosses that same river. The one pass through the mountains into Kaldrun is the **Pale Pass**, and the
Crown keeps soldiers on it.

The borders are not tidy lines. They follow a ridge, a river, the edge of a wood or a marsh, which is why
the kingdom is the shape it is.
