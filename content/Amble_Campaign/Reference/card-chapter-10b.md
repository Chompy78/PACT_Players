# Chapter 10b — Tea Leaves

Chapter 10b of the PACT campaign is titled "Tea Leaves". It is the summer chapter. Asked what happened in Chapter 10b, or for a summary of Chapter 10b, this is that chapter. Chapter 10b and "Tea Leaves" are the same chapter.

The player characters are 17 years old in Chapter 10b.

What happened in Chapter 10b: The morning after the landing, the party decides to put the Ropewalks tavern off until dark, and goes to lunch at Dela's to watch Nessa. Skylar follows Nessa to the Anchor Light shrine on the Low Quay, where Nessa is training as a healer under the keeper, Ysolt. Fenwick and Anders go in with Moss hidden as a spider and make a donation; Ysolt sees through their story but sends them politely on their way. Moss searches the shrine and finds nothing but ordinary stores. The party then goes to the Low Quay warehouse, where Halder Kest is among the people queuing for small boxes of fishing tackle. Fenwick, giving the name Benwick, asks for a sensitive item and is led into an office by a man called Blake; the box holds only tea leaves, and Blake and a second man, Jack, beat him. Moss, as a bear, and then Anders and Caspian, as a dire wolf, get into the warehouse and out again with Fenwick, and Skylar draws the crowd away from the door. Nobody dies and nobody is captured. Peter stops Anders on his way home, buys him an ale, and demands that the brandy and cigars from the missing box be returned or paid for with six hundred gold. Anders realises Moss took the box; Moss owns up, and the box and its contents are gone. A search of Halder's house finds only fishing tackle and an old worn charm. Wren brings word that the charms will land on the next shipment, tomorrow night. Factor Slate tells the party to rest and then seize the charms, with no killing. Moss, who drank the brandy and smoked the cigars, cannot sleep.

The player characters present in Chapter 10b are Caspian, Skylar, Anders Pipeleaf, Fenwick Copperkettle and Moss Stormspud.

Archer is not present in Chapter 10b.
