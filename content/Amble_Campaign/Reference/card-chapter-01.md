# Chapter 1 — Spring: The Broken Charm

Chapter 1 of the PACT campaign is titled "Spring: The Broken Charm". It is the spring chapter. Asked what happened in Chapter 1, or for a summary of Chapter 1, this is that chapter. Chapter 1 and "Spring: The Broken Charm" are the same chapter.

The player characters are 8 years old in Chapter 1.

What happened in Chapter 1: The party are eight years old. Bram fights Wren at the council tree over his grandfather's stolen wooden charm. The group finds the charm, returns it, rescues an injured fey creature from a hunting party, and votes Wren into their cubby.

The player characters present in Chapter 1 are Caspian, Skylar, Moss Stormspud, Fenwick Copperkettle and Archer.

Anders Pipeleaf is not present in Chapter 1.
