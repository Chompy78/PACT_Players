# Reference

Short, plain reference pages for the people and chapters of the Prelude — one page per character and one
per chapter, each stating the basics in a single place.

These exist mainly so the campaign assistant can answer straightforward questions ("who is Bram?", "how old
are they in Chapter 2?") accurately. The story itself lives under [[The_Story_So_Far/index|The Story So Far]];
these pages are a quick lookup, not a substitute for reading it.

Everything here is drawn from the published chapters and the players' own character sheets. Nothing on these
pages is ahead of where the published story has reached.
