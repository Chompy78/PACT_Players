# The Mentors of Amble

Amble has eleven mentors. Each teaches one path, and takes on apprentices from the village.

- Ivar Cane - Fighter mentor, and the village smith. A half-orc.
- Halla Storn - Barbarian mentor, and a huntress. A human.
- Ser Doran - Paladin mentor, a retired knight and priest. A human.
- Mother Yela - Cleric mentor, healer and hearth-witch, who runs the village shrine. A human.
- Old Rowan - Druid mentor, an ancient hedge-druid. An elf. He becomes Caspian's mentor at the end of Chapter 3.
- Sella Rook - Ranger mentor, and a road-warden. A human.
- Kess the Weaver - Rogue mentor, who deals in cloth and gossip. A halfling.
- Brother Duon - Monk mentor, a wandering ascetic. A gnome.
- Old Piper - Bard mentor, the blind storyteller of the Amble tavern. A dwarf.
- Master Verel - Wizard mentor, and a scholar. A half-elf.
- Yalen Fane - Sorcerer mentor, young and strangely lucky. A tiefling. He becomes Skylar's mentor at the end of Chapter 3.

That is the complete list of the mentors of Amble - all eleven of them.
