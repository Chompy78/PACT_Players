# Chapter 4 — Winter: The Iron Coin

Chapter 4 of the PACT campaign is titled "Winter: The Iron Coin". It is the winter chapter. Asked what happened in Chapter 4, or for a summary of Chapter 4, this is that chapter. Chapter 4 and "Winter: The Iron Coin" are the same chapter.

The player characters are 16 years old in Chapter 4.

What happened in Chapter 4: The party are sixteen. A plague called Grey Fever reaches Amble and turns adults to dust. Old Corr and Mother Yela both die. The party steal a magical pearl called a Silver Drop from the Iron Coin Guild and throw it down the village well, which saves Amble. The Guild indentures all six of them for seven years and brands them. Wren joins un-indentured. They leave for Stoneharbour.

The player characters present in Chapter 4 are Caspian, Skylar, Moss Stormspud, Fenwick Copperkettle, Anders Pipeleaf and Archer.
