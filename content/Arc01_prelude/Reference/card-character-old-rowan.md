# Old Rowan

**Old Rowan is an NPC** — a non-player character. Old Rowan is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Old Rowan is an elf. The species (or race) of Old is elf.

**Role:** Druid mentor, an ancient hedge-druid. He becomes Caspian's mentor at the end of Chapter 3. He is one of the mentors of Amble.

Stooped and lichen-grey-haired even for an elf, bark-brown weathered skin, leaning on a staff older than the village itself.
