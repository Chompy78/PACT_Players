# Sena Voll

**Sena Voll is an NPC** — a non-player character. Sena Voll is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Sena Voll is a half-elf. The species (or race) of Sena is half-elf.

**Role:** A fellow Grade I indentured worker sharing the party's barracks block, and the longest-serving of them.

**Home:** The barracks block, Guild Quarter.

Sena Voll is about 19 years old.

Older than the rest by a few hard years, with a small scratched tally on the inside of her bunk frame.

Dry, low and sparing. She doesn't waste warmth, but she doesn't lie to the new ones either.
