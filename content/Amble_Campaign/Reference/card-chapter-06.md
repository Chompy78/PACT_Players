# Chapter 6 — The Line We Crossed

Chapter 6 of the PACT campaign is titled "The Line We Crossed". It is the late summer chapter. Asked what happened in Chapter 6, or for a summary of Chapter 6, this is that chapter. Chapter 6 and "The Line We Crossed" are the same chapter.

The player characters are 16 years old in Chapter 6.

What happened in Chapter 6: Two more months of guild routine pass before a day off turns up something the guild never mentioned: the dockhands are on strike, and the party are assigned to cross the picket line in their place. Two days of hauling under jeers end in real violence -- Fenwick dunked in the harbour, Skylar turned back twice, Moss beaten back before finally breaking through -- until Anders talks the line down rather than fighting it. A special-cargo assignment turns up smuggling; guard duty brings a rat fight and a harbour-monster brawl. Sena Voll, a barracks-mate a year from finishing her indenture, refuses to cross the picket line and is gone from the barracks by the next day. In Low Quay, at a tavern called the Low Tide, they meet an information broker who introduces himself as Silas but is nicknamed Dodgy Bob on the spot, and trade a claim about goblin prisoners for a small, unidentified vial.

The player characters present in Chapter 6 are Skylar, Moss Stormspud, Fenwick Copperkettle, Caspian, Anders Pipeleaf and Archer.
