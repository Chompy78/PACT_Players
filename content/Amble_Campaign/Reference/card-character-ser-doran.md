# Ser Doran

**Ser Doran is an NPC** — a non-player character. Ser Doran is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Ser Doran is a human. The species (or race) of Ser is human.

**Role:** Paladin mentor, a retired knight and priest. He is one of the mentors of Amble.

Silver-haired but still upright, old plate kept oiled and now worn loosely over a plain robe.
