# Wren

**Wren is an NPC** — a non-player character. Wren is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Wren is a tiefling. The species (or race) of Wren is tiefling.

Wren is not a human or a halfling.

**Role:** The party's closest friend in Amble. She draws constantly and knows everyone's business.

Her mother Mara dies between Chapter 1 and Chapter 2. After that she lives with Mother Yela at the shrine.

Wren is 15 years old in Chapter 4.

In Chapter 1 she is voted into the cubby and becomes one of the group.

In Chapter 3 she is kidnapped by Halloway the tanner and rescued at the old lime pits.

In Chapter 4 she demands to join the Iron Coin Guild and leaves Amble with the party, un-indentured.
