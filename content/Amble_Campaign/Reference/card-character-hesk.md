# Hesk

**Hesk is an NPC** — a non-player character. Hesk is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Hesk is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** An older keeper at Stoneharbour's public shrine on Shrine Hill, quick to blame outsiders for the town's hard winters.

**Home:** The public shrine, Shrine Hill, Stoneharbour.

In Chapter 8 he resents Wren for reporting that he had been skimming the shrine's own donation box.

Days later Wren is found beaten outside the guild, warned by her attackers to stay away from the shrine. Hesk is never confirmed as responsible -- the party strongly suspects him, but the attackers who ambush them are unmasked as strangers, and nothing on the page ties him to the attack directly.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published Chapter 8.*
