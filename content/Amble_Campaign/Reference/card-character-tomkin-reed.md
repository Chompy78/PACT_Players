# Tomkin Reed

**Tomkin Reed is an NPC** — a non-player character. Tomkin Reed is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Tomkin Reed is a halfling. The species (or race) of Tomkin is halfling.

**Role:** A fellow Grade I indentured worker sharing the party's barracks block, known as Tam.

**Home:** The barracks block, Guild Quarter.

Tomkin Reed is about 15 years old.

Small and quick-grinned, and the easiest of the bunk room to like. Warm, funny and generous — the one who saves you a seat at mess.

In Chapter 5 he saves the party a seat at mess on their first night in the Stoneharbour barracks.
