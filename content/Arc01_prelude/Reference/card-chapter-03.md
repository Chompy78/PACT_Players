# Chapter 3 — Autumn: Who to Believe

Chapter 3 of the PACT campaign is titled "Autumn: Who to Believe". It is the autumn chapter. Asked what happened in Chapter 3, or for a summary of Chapter 3, this is that chapter. Chapter 3 and "Autumn: Who to Believe" are the same chapter.

The player characters are 14 years old in Chapter 3.

What happened in Chapter 3: The party are fourteen. Wren goes missing. They follow her sketchbooks and a burial token to Halloway the tanner, who is trafficking bodies, and rescue her at the old lime pits at dusk. Bram is scarred across the face. Afterwards they are formally acknowledged into their apprenticeships at the autumn festival.

The player characters present in Chapter 3 are Caspian, Skylar, Moss Stormspud and Anders Pipeleaf.

Fenwick Copperkettle and Archer are not present in Chapter 3.
