# Halloway

**Halloway is an NPC** — a non-player character. Halloway is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

The published pages do not record what species Halloway is. If you are asked, the honest answer is that it is not stated anywhere the campaign assistant can see.

**Role:** The tanner of Amble, whose trade kept its distance from politer noses.

In Chapter 3 he is exposed as a body-trafficker: he kidnapped Wren, kept a ledger titled Goods, and was caught at the old lime pits at dusk.

He surrenders after Bram lands one more swing, and the council sends him to a larger town for sentencing.

*Reference page for the campaign assistant. Source: Published chapters 1-4.*
