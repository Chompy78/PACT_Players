# Corley Finn

**Corley Finn is an NPC** — a non-player character. Corley Finn is not a player character, and is not one of the six player characters in the party.

**Species:** Corley Finn is a half-elf. The species (or race) of Corley is half-elf.

Corley is not a human, a halfling, an elf or a gnome.

**Role:** A fellow Grade I indentured worker sharing the party's barracks block.

**Home:** The barracks block, Guild Quarter.

Corley Finn is about 20 years old.

Well-fed for the first time in his life and visibly grateful for it; keeps his kit immaculate.

Earnest and loyal, and a little defensive of the Guild when the others grumble.

In Chapter 5 he is the first of the bunk room to introduce himself, is proud of having made grade two a couple of months earlier, and warns the party off going drinking. He is indentured like the rest of them.
